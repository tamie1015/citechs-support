Accounts & passwords

I forgot my school account password. What should I do?

Try the platform's "Forgot password" option using your school email. If that fails, submit a support request with your full name, role (student/teacher/staff), and ID number so CITECHS can verify and assist.

I can't sign in to Google Classroom or school email.

Confirm you are using your school email (not a personal Gmail). Check Caps Lock, try another browser or Incognito/Private mode, and clear cached passwords. If the error continues, open a ticket and include the exact error message.

My account looks locked or disabled.

Do not keep guessing passwords. Submit a request marked High priority and note when the lock started. CITECHS will review account status with the school's policies.

WiFi & network

I can't connect to School WiFi.
  1. Toggle Airplane Mode off/on, then retry WiFi.
  2. Forget the school network and reconnect with the correct credentials.
  3. Restart your device.
  4. Try another nearby access point or room.

If many people in the same room are offline, report an outage with building and room number.

WiFi connects but websites won't load.

Check if only some sites fail (filtering) or all sites fail. Try switching DNS/browser temporarily isn't required-report the issue with your device type, room, and time it started so CITECHS can investigate.

Devices

My school laptop won't turn on.

Plug into a known-good charger for at least 15 minutes, then try powering on. If the charging light never appears or it still won't boot, submit a ticket with asset/serial info if available and where the device can be picked up.

The classroom projector has no signal.
  • Confirm the projector is powered on and not in standby.
  • Check the HDMI/cable connection on both ends.
  • Select the correct input source on the projector remote.
  • Try another cable or laptop if available.

Still blank? Request classroom AV support and include the room number.

The printer won't print.

Check paper, toner/ink, and error lights. Confirm you selected the correct printer and that the device is online. For school printers, include the printer location/name in your ticket.

Classroom tech

I need software installed for class.

Submit a request with the software name, version (if known), which devices/rooms need it, and the date needed. Installs follow school approval policies and may take time-request early when possible.

Screen casting / wireless display isn't working.

Ensure the teacher device and display are on the school network. Restart both devices, then retry. Include room number and device types in your ticket if it still fails.

Tickets & support

How do I request help from CITECHS?

Use the Request Support form. Include a clear title, category, location, and a detailed description. The more detail you provide, the faster we can help.

What counts as an urgent request?

Examples: active class cannot teach because of AV failure, school-wide outage, exam-day system failure, or security-related account lockouts. Mark priority carefully so urgent classroom needs are not delayed.

How do I track my ticket or reopen the chat?

Keep your ticket ID from the confirmation screen. Go to Track ticket, enter that ID and the same email you used on the form, then open the private chat.

Printing

I need to print from a school computer.

Select the labeled school printer for your lab or office, check paper and error lights, then print a test page. If the job stays queued, restart the computer and try again. Include the printer name and room in a ticket if it still fails.

Safe computing

I received a suspicious email asking for my password.

CITECHS and school offices will never ask you to send your password by email or chat. Do not click unexpected links or attachments. Report the message in a High-priority ticket so the team can review it.
