What we offer

CITECHS Services

Support for everyday learning technology across Calamba Integrated School.

Hardware troubleshooting

Diagnosis and basic repair guidance for school-issued or classroom devices.

  • Laptops, Chromebooks, and tablets
  • Projectors, speakers, and interactive displays
  • Printers and scanners
  • Charging carts and accessories

Accounts & digital access

Help accessing school platforms used for teaching and learning.

  • School email / Google Workspace accounts
  • Password reset assistance (with verification)
  • Learning management system access
  • Shared drive and classroom permissions

Network & connectivity

Support when you cannot get online or stay connected on campus.

  • School WiFi connection help
  • Reporting outages by building or room
  • Device network settings checks
  • Guidance for school-approved devices

Classroom technology

Setup and assistance so lessons can run without tech interruptions.

  • AV setup for presentations
  • Software installation requests (approved apps)
  • Screen casting / display mirroring help
  • Event and lab technology support

Training & guidance

Short walkthroughs and self-help resources for common tools.

  • How-to guides in the Knowledge Base
  • Tips for teachers and staff
  • Safe computing reminders for students

Service notes

  • Support is prioritized by urgency (classroom in session, exams, outages first).
  • Personal devices may receive limited guidance; school-owned equipment is prioritized.
  • Software installs follow school policies and approved lists.
